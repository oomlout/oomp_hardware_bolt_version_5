# hardware_nut_m6_2020_t_nut

APPROXIMATE VISUAL MODEL: Fasenco 20-series hammer T-nut pattern

Dimension status: approximate_visual_model

M3/M6 not in this supplier 20-series table; visual adaptation of M4/M5. M6 body widened to 8 mm to retain thread wall. Plan corner cuts, shoulder and grip grooves simplified; neck height 1 mm selected visually. Profile-fit not certified.
